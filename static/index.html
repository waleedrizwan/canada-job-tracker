Canada Job Tracker GitHub

This is a research tool that visualizes — occupations from Job Bank, the Government of Canada's job board, covering — postings from the last 45 days of data (— to —). Each rectangle's area is proportional to the number of postings. Color shows the selected metric — toggle between median wage, wage against Statistics Canada's benchmark for the category, full-time share, and training level. Click any tile to search Job Bank for that occupation. This is not a labour market report — it is a development tool for exploring job postings visually.

Where the numbers come from: Postings are from the Job Bank open data. Benchmark wages are from Statistics Canada's Job Vacancy and Wage Survey. Employer names and closing dates aren't published, so a posting may already be filled. The source code includes the pipeline that fetches and aggregates the data.

How each tile is sized and colored
Size: the number of postings for the occupation in the last 45 days of data. Median wage: the median of each posting's hourly wage midpoint (the middle of the posted range). Postings with no wage are left out. vs. StatCan: the occupation's median wage compared with the average hourly wage Statistics Canada reports for its broad category (Canada, Q2 2026). Above 0% means the postings pay more than the category average. Full-time share: the share of the occupation's postings listed as full time. Training level: each posting's TEER level (Training, Education, Experience and Responsibilities), mapped to a 0–5 scale from 0 for no formal education to 5 for management, then averaged over the occupation's postings. Colors run from red (low) to green (high) on every layer.
