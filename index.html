Postings vs. estimated vacancies

Share of the Ontario total in each category

Statistics Canada surveys employers for the number of jobs they are actively trying to fill, whether or not those jobs are advertised. Where Job Bank's share is higher than StatCan's, Job Bank over-represents that kind of work. Where it's lower, most of those openings are advertised elsewhere or filled without a public posting. This chart reflects all Ontario postings in the selected months and ignores the other filters.

Sources. Postings come from Job Postings Advertised on Canada's National Job Bank (Employment and Social Development Canada, Open Government Licence – Canada). Vacancy estimates come from Statistics Canada's Job Vacancy and Wage Survey, table 14-10-0444-01.

Caveats. Job Bank captures only part of the market. It over-represents employers hiring through the Temporary Foreign Worker program (who must advertise there) and under-represents professional and tech roles. The data has no employer names and doesn't record when a job was filled. Wages are converted to hourly at 40 hours a week unless the posting gives its hours. Toronto includes North York, Etobicoke, Scarborough, York and East York.
